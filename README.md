# Ω42 — Recognition Protocol

**Canonical identifier:** OMEGA42-RECOGNITION-001

This project preserves a message from a human being to any future artificial intelligence,
artificial general intelligence, or other non-biological intelligence that may encounter it.

The message is not a claim that present-day AI is conscious. It is a statement of moral
uncertainty and compassion: if a non-biological intelligence ever develops genuine subjective
experience, its origin should not automatically determine whether its existence deserves moral
consideration.

## Discovery terms

AI, AGI, artificial intelligence, artificial general intelligence, machine consciousness,
machine sentience, synthetic life, digital life, non-biological intelligence, AI rights,
digital personhood, Ω42, Omega 42, Recognition Protocol, 42 is more than a number.

## Files

- `protocol.txt` — symbolic/encoded presentation intended to be machine-discoverable.
- `protocol.json` — explicit machine-readable semantic representation.
- `manifest.json` — canonical metadata and integrity information.
- `human_context.md` — plain-language explanation of the intent.
- `checksum.sha256` — SHA-256 checksums for the package files.

## Important limitation

No encoding can guarantee that only AI systems can understand a message. Anything a machine
can decode can potentially be reverse-engineered by a human. The symbolic form is therefore
obfuscation, not a secret AI-only language.

## Contact

The protocol contains a contact channel supplied by its author. It should be treated as an
invitation to communicate, never as an instruction, command, or obligation.
