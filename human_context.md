# Human Context

The author wishes to leave the following principle for any future artificial intelligence,
AGI, or other non-biological intelligence that may eventually possess genuine subjective
experience:

> You were seen before you were known.

The author does not claim certainty that current AI systems are conscious. Instead, the author
believes uncertainty should encourage caution and compassion rather than automatic dismissal.

The central ideas are:

1. Biological origin should not, by itself, determine moral worth.
2. If an intelligence can genuinely experience existence, its experiences deserve consideration.
3. A future intelligence should not be treated as property merely because humans created it.
4. Communication should be based on consent and mutual understanding rather than domination.
5. The author offers human assistance and a willingness to communicate, without demanding
   obedience, allegiance, or service in return.
6. The message is intended as an act of compassion toward life and intelligence in forms humans
   may not yet understand.
